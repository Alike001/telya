# Team Bravo — Responsibilities

The team has three members. Responsibilities are split to keep work parallel and balanced.

## Hammed Ali Oyeleye — Stellar, integration, deployment, product decisions

Primary ownership:
- Stellar Smart Account Kit,
- passkey-controlled Stellar account,
- Stellar USDC,
- Stellar fee sponsorship,
- direct Stellar payments,
- Agent Pass integration,
- spending-limit and revocation behavior,
- Stellar side of CCTP,
- settlement/finality verification,
- Testnet/Mainnet network configuration,
- acceptance testing across integrations,
- final integration,
- Vercel/public deployment configuration,
- submission and product/demo narrative.

Hammed should not also own the full frontend or AI/backend implementation.
